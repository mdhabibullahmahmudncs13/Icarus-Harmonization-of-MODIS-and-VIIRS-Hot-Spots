    The problem

    In 2012 the count doubled. The fires didn’t.

    MODIS has watched the planet since 2000. VIIRS joined in 2012 and resolves roughly three times as many hot spots inside the same cell. Left alone, the record shows a step that belongs to the instrument, not to the burning.

    Raw total, 2010–11 → 2013–14 5.42× 34.0 → 184.1 detections / day
    MODIS-only activity control 0.92× the burning itself barely moved

    A sensor-driven jump, measured on the real archive — not a simulation.

    How it works · 01–02

    Acquire, then speak one language.

    Detections come from NASA FIRMS: the MODIS and VIIRS S-NPP / NOAA-20 archive products, plus NOAA-21. Each carries its own columns and its own confidence scale, so the first job is harmony of form.

    • 01

      Acquire

      Chunked, resumable downloads into a local parquet cache, with retries for NASA throttling and a cache that never stores an error page.

    • 02

      Normalize

      One schema, one documented confidence mapping, one 0.25° grid — then every day collapses to cell-days, the unit the whole record is counted in.

    How it works · 03–04

    Harmonize, then serve it straight.

    Harmonization never rewrites a detection. It corrects the count, and it says out loud which correction it applied.

    • 03

      Harmonize

      A per-cell baseline and a multiplicative calibration applied to VIIRS-anchored bins, so one sensor’s density stops standing in for activity.

    • 04

      Serve

      FastAPI over a frozen JSON contract, read-only, every payload carrying its source and parameter hash — and a coverage tag on every bin:

      • MODIS
      • BRIDGE
      • VIIRS_CAL
      • NONE

    The result

    The step shrinks. It does not vanish — and we say so.

    On the real archive, harmonization cuts the sensor-driven jump by about a third and lifts linear agreement with the overlap period. It does not pretend the two sensors are the same instrument.

    Step, raw → harmonized 5.42× → 3.77× still above 1 — the residual is real
    Pearson agreement, 15-year overlap 0.779 → 0.823 4,665 days · ratio 6.54 → 4.02

    Every figure on the panel opens the JSON it came from.

    Validation · E1–E9

    Negative results ship too.

    Nine experiments were pre-registered with their decision rules, then run and published — including the two that fell short. Nothing was tuned after the fact to look better.

    • E1 Artificial step collapse 3.15× → 1.16×
    • E2 Calibration residual offset 1.61 → 0.39 · target 0.25 — partial
    • E8 Burned area, 0.25° cells r² 0.342 · target 0.5 — partial
    • E9 Offline cost 216 MiB cache · 1.30 s payload
